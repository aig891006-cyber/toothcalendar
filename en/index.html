Newborn

Born with a tooth?A tooth at birth or in the first month is rare. Most are simply watched, but a loose one needs a dental check.Wiping the gums before teeth appearWiping the gums with clean, damp gauze after feeds is the first step toward a lifelong brushing habit.White bumps and white patches in a newborn's mouthTiny white bumps in the mouth usually go away on their own. A coating that won't wipe off may be thrush.

Preschool (3–6)

The first wiggly tooth: losing baby teeth beginsBetween six and seven, lower front teeth loosen and fall out. "Shark teeth" usually sort themselves out.Sleeping with the mouth open, and snoringMouth breathing and snoring can affect the bite and how the face grows. Don't brush it off. Find the cause.Six-year molars fully in? Time for sealantsOnce the six-year molars are fully in, seal the grooves right away. It's a top way to prevent molar cavities.
